DELIVERY & RETURNS

Shipping & Returns

Shipping

  • Dispatch. Orders are prepared and dispatched from County Cork, Ireland within 2 to 3 working days.
  • Worldwide, tracked. We ship internationally with a tracked service; you'll receive tracking once your piece is on its way.
  • Delivery times vary by destination once dispatched, typically a few days within Ireland and the UK, and longer for international destinations.
  • Duties & taxes. Orders outside the EU may be subject to import duties or taxes set by the destination country. These are the recipient's responsibility.

Returns

  • 14 days. If a piece isn't right, you may return it within 14 days of delivery.
  • Original condition. Returned items must be unused and in their original condition and packaging. For reasons of hygiene, brushes and razors that have been used cannot be returned unless faulty.
  • How to start a return. Contact us with your order details and we'll guide you through it.
  • Refunds are issued to your original payment method once the returned piece is received and inspected.

Faulty or damaged pieces

Every piece is inspected before it leaves the workshop, but if something arrives damaged or develops a fault, get in touch as soon as possible and we'll put it right.

A note on one of one pieces

Because each piece is unique, natural variation in grain, colour and figure is expected and celebrated. It is not a fault. The exact piece shown is the piece you receive.

QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

FAQ

What does "one of one" mean?

Each piece is made individually from natural materials and will never be repeated. The exact piece shown in the photographs is the piece you receive. Its grain, colour and character are unique to it.

Are the products really handmade?

Yes. Every object is shaped, finished and assembled by hand in a small workshop in County Cork, Ireland, in small numbers.

Where do you ship, and how long does it take?

We ship worldwide with tracked delivery. Orders are dispatched from County Cork within 2 to 3 working days. See Shipping & Returns for full details.

What is your returns policy?

Unused pieces can be returned within 14 days of delivery in their original condition. Full details are on the Shipping & Returns page.

How do I care for my piece?

Care is simple and depends on the material. See the Care Guide for brushes, razors, writing instruments, leather and tweed.

Is checkout secure?

Yes. Payment is handled by Shopify's secure, PCI compliant hosted checkout. We never see or store your card details.

Do you offer gifting?

Pieces arrive boxed and ready to give, with a care card. For ideas, see the Gift Guide. Remember, each piece is one of one, so it won't wait.

Can I commission a bespoke piece?

Depending on materials and workshop schedule, sometimes yes. Get in touch and tell us what you have in mind.

LOOKING AFTER YOUR PIECE

Care Guide

Every Barbermatic piece is made from natural materials and finished by hand. With a little care it will age gracefully and last a lifetime. General rule: keep pieces dry, away from prolonged direct heat and sunlight, and clean gently.

Shaving brushes

  • Rinse the knot thoroughly in warm water after each use.
  • Shake out the excess water and gently squeeze (never wring) the bristles.
  • Stand or hang to dry at room temperature, away from radiators and direct heat.
  • Avoid soaking the handle; prolonged submersion can affect the finish over time.
  • A new badger knot may shed a few hairs and carry a faint natural scent for the first few uses. Both settle quickly.

Safety & cartridge razors

  • Rinse and dry the handle and head after every shave.
  • Store somewhere dry between uses; don't leave it sitting in water.
  • Occasionally treat a wooden handle with a little wax or oil to protect the finish.
  • Replace blades regularly for a comfortable, hygienic shave.

Writing instruments

  • Wipe the barrel with a soft, dry cloth. Avoid solvents and abrasives.
  • Keep away from prolonged direct heat or sunlight, which can affect natural timbers.
  • For fountain pens, flush the nib and feed periodically with clean water.
  • Cap the pen when not in use to keep the nib from drying.

Leather & tweed

  • Wipe clean with a slightly damp cloth and allow to air dry naturally.
  • Condition leather occasionally to keep it supple and to deepen its patina.
  • Keep away from prolonged damp and direct heat.

Turned wood & personal objects

  • Dust with a soft cloth. An occasional touch of wax keeps the finish deep.
  • Handle is part of the pleasure. Natural oils from the hand only improve the timber over time.
